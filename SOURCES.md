- MSU lists the BSc as 2022, while the user's graduation post is dated November 2023. Both facts are distinguished.
- The current university profile uses Assistant Lecturer / Staff Development Fellow. The site uses that title rather than the older “Research Assistant Lecturer.”
- GeoConnect's own page says co-founder; that wording is preferred to other sources' founder wording.
- Higherlife role and start year follow the supplied site and recent user context. Update if the role has changed.
- Public pages mention additional professional service and an industry award, but dates and award details were not sufficiently clear to add another award entry.
- Original portfolio demonstrations, sample-transportation research, training projects and school education can be expanded into additional case studies once descriptive results and current project URLs are supplied. The redesign prioritises documented flagship work over an undifferentiated gallery.
- No automatic CV-download button is included because a current, approved public CV was not supplied.
- No unsupported reach statistics, testimonials, performance percentages or claims of health outcomes are included.
